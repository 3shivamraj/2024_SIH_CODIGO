# 🚀 Mentor Connect

## 🌐 Bridging Students and Mentors Through Technology

**Mentor Connect** is a smart education-focused platform designed to help students connect with mentors for **career guidance, doubt solving, and skill development** through a simple, structured, and technology-driven system. The platform aims to reduce the gap between students and experienced mentors by enabling one-to-one interaction and guided learning.

## 🧠 Problem Statement

Many students struggle to get:

* Proper career guidance
* One-to-one doubt solving
* Access to experienced mentors
* Structured mentorship at the right time

Existing platforms are often complex, costly, or inaccessible for students from diverse backgrounds.

---

## 💡 Solution – Mentor Connect

Mentor Connect provides a **centralized digital platform** where:

* Students can find and connect with mentors
* Mentors can guide students based on skills and interests
* One-to-one interaction is enabled for personalized learning

The system focuses on **simplicity, accessibility, and scalability**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 👤 **Student & Mentor Registration**
  Separate roles for students and mentors

* 📅 **Mentor Booking System**
  Students can request sessions with mentors

* 🎥 **One-to-One Interaction**
  Live interaction for doubt solving and guidance

* 📚 **Learning Support**
  Guidance related to careers, skills, and academics

* 🧑‍💼 **Admin Management**
  Admin can manage users, mentors, and requests
